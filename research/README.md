# Research Workspace

This hierarchy separates evidence, student thinking, decisions, protocols, and generated results.

```text
research/
├── reading/       paper queue and one note per paper
├── literature/    cross-paper evidence matrix
├── questions/     open research questions and hypotheses
├── decisions/     student-approved decisions and rationale
├── protocols/     frozen evaluation plans
├── resources/     framework and learning maps
└── meetings/      mentor/reviewer notes and actions
experiments/
├── configs/       versioned experiment configurations
└── README.md      experiment naming and provenance rules
results/
└── README.md      generated-output policy
```

## Rule

Agent summaries are scouting aids. Important claims must be checked against the primary paper, and every scientific decision is recorded by the student in `research/decisions/decision-log.md`.
